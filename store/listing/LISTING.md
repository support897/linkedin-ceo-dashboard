## Full description (both stores)

> LinkedIn CEO Dashboard is a personal dashboard for one person's LinkedIn
> work. It shows the week at a glance and keeps the follow-up work in one
> place.
>
> What it does:
> - Shows this week's LinkedIn posts with their comment and like counts.
> - Lists outreach leads and where each conversation is up to, with the
>   next step due.
> - Tracks freebie requests: who asked, what they asked for, and whether
>   it has been sent.
> - Holds a private photo vault for personal post photos.
> - Shows worker status and weekly roll-ups for the team behind the posts.
> - Can send reminder notifications, only if you turn them on.
>
> The app reads and writes its own private database. It does not post to
> LinkedIn for you, and it does not show impressions or reach figures that
> LinkedIn does not provide.

## Content rating notes

- No user-generated public content, no chat with strangers, no ads, no
  gambling, no violent or sexual content, no location tracking.
- Google Play IARC questionnaire: answer "No" to every content category;
  the app is a private productivity tool. Expected rating: **Everyone**.
- Apple age rating questionnaire: "None" for every category in the
  standard matrix. Expected rating: **4+**.
- Apple "App Privacy" and Google Play "Data safety" answers must match
  `privacy-policy.html` in this folder: data is the owner's own business
  records in her own database; photos she uploads; a push endpoint if she
  enables reminders. Nothing is sold. No third-party advertising or
  analytics SDKs are included.
