Privacy Policy — LinkedIn CEO Dashboard

Last updated: 13 October 2026

LinkedIn CEO Dashboard is a private, single-user dashboard. It is used by one person to run her own LinkedIn work. This page says, plainly, what the app stores and what it does not do.

What the app stores

The app stores its records in a private Supabase database that belongs to the app owner. The records are:

  • LinkedIn post records and their comment and like counts.
  • Outreach lead records: names, LinkedIn profile links, business details, conversation stage, notes and message drafts.
  • Freebie request records: the name of the person who asked, what they asked for, and whether it was sent.
  • Photos the owner uploads to the app's photo vault, stored in the same private project.
  • Worker status records and weekly roll-ups.
  • If the owner turns on reminders, a push notification subscription (the device endpoint and keys) so reminders can be delivered.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell data, and it does not share data with advertisers.
  • It contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • It does not track location.
  • It does not post to LinkedIn or send messages on anyone's behalf.
  • It has no public accounts and no sign-up for other people.

Device storage

The app keeps its own program files on the device so it can open offline. It does not read contacts, calendars, files or other apps on the device.

Deleting data

The owner can delete records, including vault photos, from the database at any time. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy. To ask about data held in the database, contact the owner at hello@ilseplacencia.shop.
