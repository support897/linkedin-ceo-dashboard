The guard rules

  • 100 invites a week is the whole budget. About 20 a day, weekdays only.
  • Yes-rate under 25% for a week: stop, fix the list or the note.
  • Any LinkedIn warning or extra check: stop everything for 2 weeks.
  • Withdraw invites still unanswered after 21 days.
  • Never the same wording to a long run of people.
  • Outreach alerts ride on the same Reminders switch on Home. Turn reminders on there once, and due items also show in Needs Attention on Home.
